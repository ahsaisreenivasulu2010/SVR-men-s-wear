Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place a custom tailoring order through the SVR Men's Wear app or website, we collect only the details needed to fulfill your order: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, delivery address, PIN code, and optional clothing measurement notes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To stitch and customize your garments according to your fit preferences.
  • To contact you regarding measurements, order updates, or delivery confirmation.
  • To ship your finished clothes via India Post (Speed Post or Registered Parcel) and share your tracking number.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Delivery details (name, address, phone, and PIN code) are shared only with India Post when dispatching your parcel.
